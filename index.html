BEFORE YOU SWITCH

Both files read the same saved data (liftlog_v2) and carry the same seed version, so neither one will overwrite the other. Your log follows you between them.

Day edits are stored by weekday, not by program. Friday is Legs B on the 5-day and Pull B on the 6-day, so a Friday tweak made in one shows up wrong in the other. Pick one file for day edits and stay there.
